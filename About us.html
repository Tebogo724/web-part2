About us

Tebogo Property Enterprise History

• The organisation was The Boshomane Property on February 18, 1999, and the founder was David Boshomane. The Boshomane Property was first a real estate organisation that helped renters find apartments and townhouses in Pretoria. In 2009 it was then handed over to Tebogo Boshomane the cousin of David who then launched it into Tebogo Property Enterprise which then included helping homebuyers find the perfect house nation-wide. The organisation started because David wanted to bring improved property selling services which is accurate and in-depth information on the desired property as well as agents who are available seven days a week to help with the viewing of the property. The organisation was doing well in the first few years until it started losing clients as it was only limited to Pretoria. It was known for being the second-best real estate organisation because of the quality of services it provided. After the rebrand, it was recognized as the nation’s best property organisation with Tebogo receiving the top 30 under 30 awards for the company’s success. In 2019 the organisation has sold over 500000 properties in the first three months of the year. The organisation now has agents across all provinces of the country.

Mission

Our mission is to have well experienced agents and be Africa’s largest and best property organisation.

Vision
